Worship Checklist

Daily Puja Samagri List (नित्य पूजन सामग्री)

A complete, easy-to-follow checklist of essential items required for daily morning and evening worship at home mandir.

Complete Daily Worship Checklist

1. Lamp & Light (दीपक व प्रकाश): • Brass or clay Diya
• Pure Cow Ghee or Sesame oil
• Cotton wicks (Rui Batti)
• Camphor (Kapur) for Aarti
2. Incense & Fragrance (धूप-अगरबत्ती): • Natural Charcoal-Free Daily Agarbatti
• Guggul / Loban Dhoop Sticks
• Incense holder or ash catcher
3. Offerings & Tilak (रोली, चावल व पुष्प): • Kumkum / Roli & Chandan paste
• Akshata (unbroken clean rice grains)
• Fresh flowers & Tulsi leaves
• Gangajal or fresh water in Kalash
4. Bhog & Prasad (नैवेद्य): • Mishri, Jaggery-Gram, Boondi, or fresh fruits
• Pure drinking water in a small vessel
