Katra Bazar Assembly Constituency (AC No. 297)

Paraspur Block is geographically situated within the Katra Bazar Vidhan Sabha constituency (No. 297) of Uttar Pradesh. The constituency encompasses Paraspur Block, Colonelganj tehsil areas, and Katra Bazar. It falls under the Kaiserganj Lok Sabha Parliamentary Constituency.

Gram Panchayat & Civic Helplines

The 3-tier Panchayati Raj system in Paraspur Block ensures citizen services are accessible at the grassroots level:

🏛️ Block Development Office (BDO) Oversees PMAY-G housing schemes, MNREGA rural labor deployment, and village sanitation projects.
🏡 64 Gram Pradhans Elected village heads managing local drinking water pumps, street lights, and Gram Sabha meetings.
📞 National Voter Helpline (1950) Free EC hotline for voter ID registration, address changes, and election polling station queries.
