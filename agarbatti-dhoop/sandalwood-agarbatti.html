Sandalwood Agarbatti (चंदन अगरबत्ती)

Experience the cooling, divine fragrance of pure Mysore Sandalwood. Renowned in Vedic scriptures for enhancing concentration and purifying spiritual spaces.

Benefits & Traditional Use of Sandalwood Incense

Sandalwood (Chandan) is revered in Sanatana Dharma as a sacred offering to deities. Burning pure Sandalwood agarbatti cools the environment, calms an agitated mind, and creates a sanctified atmosphere for Gayatri Japa, Vishnu Sahasranama, and daily morning prayers.

  • Cooling & Soothing: Natural sandalwood oil reduces mental stress and fatigue.
  • Charcoal-Free Formulation: Made from pure sandalwood wood powder and natural binding resins.
  • 45-55 Minute Burn Duration: Long steady burn suitable for complete morning puja ceremonies.

Recommended Deities for Sandalwood Offering:

Sandalwood is particularly sacred for Lord Vishnu, Sri Krishna, Lord Ganesha, and Lord Shiva. Pair with our Shiv Puja Incense Guide.
