Shri Hanuman Chalisa

The 40 poetic verses celebrating Lord Hanuman's matchless devotion, strength, wisdom, and protective grace. Authored by Goswami Tulsidas, whose early spiritual journey began at Sukarkhet (Paraspur/Gonda).

Spiritual Significance & Regional Connection

Shri Hanuman Chalisa (श्री हनुमान चालीसा) is a revered 40-verse hymn composed in 16th-century Awadhi Hindi by the saint-poet Goswami Tulsidas. According to regional tradition and historical manuscripts, Tulsidas spent his formidable early years receiving Ramkatha initiation at Sukarkhet Paska—the sacred confluence of Sarayu and Ghaghara in Paraspur block, Gonda.

Reciting the Hanuman Chalisa instills courage (Bala), wisdom (Buddhi), and pure devotion (Vidya). It is believed to remove anxiety, grant protection from negative influences, and help overcome obstacles in daily life.

Step-by-Step Instructions for Chanting Hanuman Chalisa

1

Preparation & Cleanliness (Shuchita)

Take a bath in the morning or evening. Wear clean yellow or orange clothes. Sit on a clean woollen mat (Aasana) facing East or North.

2

Lighting Diya & Offering Prasadam

Light a ghee or mustard oil lamp (diya) in front of an idol or picture of Lord Hanuman. Offer fresh water, red flowers, and Boondi, Laddoo, or Jaggery-Gram (Gur-Chana) as Bhog.

3

Sankalpa & Chanting Frequency

Offer a heartfelt prayer (Sankalpa). Recite the Hanuman Chalisa 1, 7, 11, or 100 times depending on your resolution. Focus with devotion and steady pronunciation.

4

Closing Aarti & Ksama Yachana

Perform Hanuman Ji Ki Aarti, distribute the sanctified prasad, and bow respectfully to seek divine blessings.

Shri Hanuman Chalisa (Full Text & Meaning)

Complete Awadhi Dohas and 40 Chaupais with Hindi & English Verse Explanations.

Opening Dohas (प्रारंभिक दोहा)

श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि।
बरनऊँ रघुबर बिमल जसु जो दायकु फल चारि॥

Meaning: Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust of the lotus feet of Holy Guru, I narrate the pure glory of Lord Ramachandra, which bestows the four fruits of life (Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha).

बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके सुमिरौं पवन-कुमार।
बल बुधि बिद्या देहु मोहिं हरहु कलेस बिकार॥

Meaning: Knowing myself to be ignorant, I meditate upon Hanuman, the son of the Wind God. O Lord! Grant me physical strength, wisdom, and knowledge, and relieve all my afflictions and shortcomings.

40 Chaupais (४० चौपाइयाँ)

Verses 1 – 4

जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर। जय कपीस तिहुँ लोक उजागर॥१॥
रामदूत अतुलित बल धामा। अंजनि-पुत्र पवनसुत नामा॥२॥
महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी। कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी॥३॥
कंचन बरन बिराज सुबेसा। कानन कुंडल कुंचित केसा॥४॥

Meaning: Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue! Victory to the chief of monkeys who illuminates all three worlds. You are Lord Rama’s envoy, abode of incomparable strength, son of Anjani, and known as the Wind-Son. O mighty hero, possessing lightning strength, remover of negative thoughts, and companion of righteous wisdom! You shine in gold-hued attire, adorned with sparkling earrings and curly locks.

Verses 5 – 8

हाथ बज्र औ ध्वजा बिराजै। काँधे मूँज जनेऊ साजै॥५॥
संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन। तेज प्रताप महा जग बन्दन॥६॥
बिद्यावान गुनी अति चातुर। राम काज करिबे को आतुर॥७॥
प्रभु चरित्र सुनिबे को रसिया। राम लखन सीता मन बसिया॥८॥

Meaning: In your hands rest the mace (Vajra) and banner; your shoulder is adorned with the sacred thread (Janeu). You are the incarnation of Shiva, son of Kesari; your radiant splendor is venerated by the entire universe. You are learned, virtuous, extremely clever, and ever-eager to fulfill Lord Rama's tasks. You delight in listening to Lord Rama’s deeds and dwell eternally in the hearts of Rama, Lakshmana, and Sita.

Verses 9 – 12

सूक्ष्म रूप धरि सियहिं दिखावा। बिकट रूप धरि लंक जरावा॥९॥
भीम रूप धरि असुर संहारे। रामचंद्र के काज संवारे॥१०॥
लॉय सजीवन लखन जियाये। श्रीरघुबीर हरषि उर लाये॥११॥
रघुपति कीन्ही बहुत बड़ाई। तुम मम प्रिय भरतहि सम भाई॥१२॥

Meaning: Assuming a microscopic form, you appeared before Sita Ji; assuming a colossal form, you burnt Lanka. Taking a formidable form, you destroyed the demons and accomplished Lord Rama's divine mission. You brought the Sanjeevani herb to revive Lakshmana, filling Lord Rama with immense joy. Lord Rama praised you greatly, saying, "You are as dear to me as my own brother Bharata!"

Verses 24 – 27 (Famous Healing & Protective Verses)

भूत पसाच निकट नहिं आवै। महाबीर जब नाम सुनावै॥२४॥
नासै रोग हरै सब पीरा। जपत निरंतर हनुमत बीरा॥२५॥
संकट तें हनुमान छुड़ावै। मन क्रम बचन ध्यान जो लावै॥२६॥
सब पर राम तपस्वी राजा। तिन के काज सकल तुम साजा॥२७॥

Meaning: Evil spirits and negative energies cannot approach when your holy name Mahavira is uttered. All diseases are destroyed and all pain is eradicated by constantly chanting the name of Hanuman. Hanuman releases from all hardship anyone who remembers him in thought, word, and deed. Lord Rama is the supreme ascetic King, and you effortlessly accomplish all his tasks.

Verses 36 – 40

संकट कटै मिटै सब पीरा। जो सुमिरै हनुमत बलबीरा॥३६॥
जै जै जै हनुमान गोसाईं। कृपा करहु गुरु देव की नाईं॥३७॥
जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई। छूटहि बंदि महा सुख होई॥३८॥
जो यह पड़ै हनुमान चालीसा। होय सिद्धि साखी गौरीसा॥३९॥
तुलसीदास सदा हरि चेरा। कीजै नाथ हृदय मँह डेरा॥४०॥

Meaning: All difficulties vanish and all pain dissolves for one who remembers the powerful Hanuman. Hail, hail, hail Lord Hanuman! Bestow your grace upon me as a divine Guru. Whoever recites this Chalisa one hundred times will be freed from all bondage and attain supreme bliss. Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa attains perfection—to this Lord Shiva (Gaurisa) stands witness. Tulsidas is ever the servant of Lord Hari; O Lord! Dwell forever in my heart.

Closing Doha (समापन दोहा)

पवनतनय संकट हरन मंगल मूरति रूप।
राम लखन सीता सहित हृदय बसहु सुर भूप॥

Meaning: O Son of Wind, dispeller of difficulties, embodiment of auspiciousness! Abide in my heart along with Lord Rama, Lakshmana, and Mother Sita.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the best time to chant Hanuman Chalisa?

The early morning (Brahma Muhurta, 4:00 AM to 6:00 AM) and evening twilight period (Sandhya Kaal) are considered most beneficial for Hanuman Chalisa recitation.

Q2. How many times should one recite Hanuman Chalisa daily?

Reciting it 1, 3, or 7 times daily brings peace and mental stability. On Tuesdays and Saturdays, chanting it 11 or 100 times fulfills special resolutions.

Q3. What is Goswami Tulsidas's connection to Paraspur / Gonda?

Tulsidas Ji studied at Sukarkhet Paska (located in Paraspur block, Gonda) under his guru Narharidas Ji. It is at Sukarkhet that he first heard the sacred Ramkatha before writing Ramcharitmanas and Chalisa.
