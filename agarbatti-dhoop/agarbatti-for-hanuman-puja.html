Favored Aromas for Lord Hanuman

During Hanuman Puja, offering the appropriate fragrance enhances devotional vibrations and helps focus the mind during Sundarkand or Hanuman Chalisa chanting.

1. Jasmine / Chameli & Mogra Agarbatti Chameli oil (jasmine oil) is traditionally mixed with orange Sindoor to offer a Chola to Lord Hanuman. Burning Mogra Agarbatti creates an authentic temple atmosphere.
2. Pure Guggul Dhoop Sticks (गुग्गुल धूप) Guggul is a powerful natural resin that dispels negative energy, fear, and bad dreams. Burning bamboo-free Dhoop Sticks during Tuesday evening aarti is highly auspicious.
3. Sandalwood (Chandan) Incense Cooling Sandalwood Agarbatti provides peace and balance after intense chanting sessions.
