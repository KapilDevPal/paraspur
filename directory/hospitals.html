Frequently Asked Questions: Healthcare in Paraspur

Where is the nearest 24/7 government emergency hospital in Paraspur?

The Community Health Center (CHC) Paraspur, located on the Main Road near the Police Station, operates 24 hours a day with emergency medical staff and ambulance access.

How far is the District Hospital from Paraspur?

For super-specialty surgeries and advanced trauma care, patients are transferred to the Gonda District Hospital (Maharshi Balark Medical College, Gonda), located approximately 26 kilometers away via the Paraspur-Gonda state highway.

How can doctors or clinics add their practice to this directory?

Practicing physicians and clinic administrators in Paraspur Block can submit their official registration, consultation timings, and emergency numbers via our Contact Page for verified free inclusion.
