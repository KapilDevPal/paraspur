Agarbatti Fragrance Types Guide

Explore the 5 primary incense scent families and find the ideal aroma profile for daily prayers, relaxation, and aromatherapy.

The 5 Major Fragrance Families

1. Woody Notes (चंदन, cedar, agarwood) Cooling, grounding, and spiritually meditative. Recommended for morning prayers. See Sandalwood Agarbatti.
2. Floral Notes (गुलाब, मोगरा, champa, lotus) Uplifting, sweet, and festive. See Rose Agarbatti & Mogra Agarbatti.
3. Resinous Notes (गुग्गुल, लोबान, sambrani, frankincense) Earthly, sacred smoke for purifying home corners. See Dhoop Sticks.
4. Herbal & Camphoraceous Notes (tulsi, neem, camphor) Air-refreshing and protective properties.
