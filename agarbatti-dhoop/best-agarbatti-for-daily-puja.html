Why Quality Agarbatti Matters for Daily Worship

Daily worship in Indian households involves lighting a ghee lamp and burning incense sticks to signify the removal of darkness and negativity. Using high-quality, natural agarbatti ensures that the smoke is non-toxic, gentle on eyes and throat, and leaves a lingering tranquil fragrance.
