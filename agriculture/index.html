Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common agricultural questions for Paraspur & Gonda farmers.

Which Mandi is closest to Paraspur for selling crops?

Colonelganj APMC Mandi is the nearest primary market for farmers in Paraspur block, followed by Gonda APMC Mandi and Nawabganj APMC Mandi. Live prices for all three are updated daily on this page.

How is the live Mandi Bhav updated on Paraspur.com?

Our system connects directly to the Open Government Data Platform (data.gov.in) API maintained by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, fetching real-time market arrivals and modal prices.

Which is the best high-yield wheat seed for Gonda district?

Recommended wheat seeds for Gonda & Eastern UP include DBW 187 (Karan Vandana), HD 2967, and PBW 550 due to high resistance against yellow rust and high grain output per acre.
