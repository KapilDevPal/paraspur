Shri Hanuman Puja Vidhi

A comprehensive, authentic step-by-step guide for performing Hanuman Puja at home, including required Samagri list, Sindoor Chola Vidhi, Bhog offerings, Chalisa recitation, and Aarti.

Puja Samagri List Recite Hanuman Chalisa Hanuman Ji Aarti

Required Puja Samagri List (पूजन सामग्री)

Essential Items: • Idol or picture of Lord Hanuman & Shri Rama
• Red/Orange Aasana (woollen sitting mat)
• Cow Ghee or Mustard Oil for lamp (diya)
• Pure Gangajal or clean water in Kalash
Offerings & Bhog: • Orange Sindoor & Chameli (Jasmine) Oil
• Red flowers, Marigold garland, Tulsi leaves
• Boondi, Besan Laddoo, or Jaggery-Gram (Gur-Chana)
• Paan (Betel leaf) with clove and cardamom

Step-by-Step Hanuman Puja Procedure

1

Pavitrikaran & Aachaman

Sit on your mat facing East or North. Sprinkle clean water over yourself and the puja area while reciting: "Om Apavitrah Pavitro Va...". Sip a drop of water thrice from your right palm.

2

Deep Prajvalan & Sankalpa

Light the Ghee/Mustard oil lamp and incense. Take water, rice, and a flower in your right hand, stating your name, place (e.g. Paraspur), and intention (Sankalpa) for the puja.

3

Sindoor & Chameli Oil Arpan (सिंदूर अर्पण)

Mix orange Sindoor with Chameli oil and gently apply to Hanuman Ji's feet and forehead. Offer red flowers, Akshata, and Tulsi leaves.

4

Reciting Hanuman Chalisa & Sundarkand

With steady devotion, recite the Shri Hanuman Chalisa 1, 7, 11, or 100 times.

5

Offering Bhog & Performing Aarti

Offer fresh Boondi or Laddoos along with Tulsi leaves. Perform Hanuman Ji Ki Aarti, seek forgiveness (Ksama Yachana), and distribute Prasad.
