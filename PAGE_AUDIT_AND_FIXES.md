## 🚫 Programmatic / Secondary Pages Marked `noindex, follow`

To prevent Google AdSense quality crawlers from penalizing the site for thin/repetitive programmatic content, the following secondary pages have been explicitly tagged with `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">`:

1. **88 Village Detail Pages** (`villages/*.html` excluding `villages/index.html`): Detail stats pages for individual villages (e.g. Aata, Abhayipur, Akohari, Pasaka, Sukarkhet, etc.). Accessible to visitors navigating from `villages/index.html`.
2. **20 Agarbatti Niche Pages** (`agarbatti-dhoop/*.html` excluding `agarbatti-dhoop/index.html`): Niche product variant guides (e.g. Sandalwood, Rose, Mogra, Dhoop Sticks). Accessible from `agarbatti-dhoop/index.html`.
