Introduction to Agarbatti and Dhoop

For thousands of years, incense (Dhupa) has played a sacred role in Indian Vedic rituals, temple worship, and daily household prayers. Burning natural agarbatti and dhoop purifies the indoor atmosphere, eliminates negative energy, aids mental focus during meditation, and creates a tranquil environment for prayer.

🌿 100% Natural & Charcoal-Free Made from dried flower petals, natural binding gums, essential oils, and herbal powders for clean, non-irritating smoke.
🕉️ Vedic Ritual Alignment Specific aromas tailored for Lord Hanuman, Shiva, and Lakshmi worship according to ancient Agamas.
⏳ Long Burn Time Engineered burn times from 45 minutes for agarbatti sticks up to 60+ minutes for dense dhoop sticks and cones.
