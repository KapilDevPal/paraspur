Incense Recommendations for Mahadev Puja

1. White Sandalwood (Chandan) Agarbatti: Lord Shiva is fond of cooling Sandalwood paste (Chandan) and Bhasma. Burn Sandalwood Incense during Rudrabhishekam.
2. Kewra & Bael Leaf Herbal Dhoop: Earthy, refreshing scents that invoke meditative calm during Monday fasts.
