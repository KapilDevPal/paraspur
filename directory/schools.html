Primary & Secondary Education in Paraspur Block

Education in Paraspur Block has witnessed significant growth with established government inter colleges providing affordable secondary schooling alongside private English-medium academies. Students from over 64 Gram Panchayats travel to Paraspur town daily for quality schooling, science streams, and competitive coaching foundations.

Frequently Asked Questions: Education in Paraspur

What are the prominent schools in Paraspur Gonda?

Tulsi Memorial Academy, Government Inter College (GIC) Paraspur, Swami Leela Shah Convent, and Shanti Devi Kamta Prasad Inter College are among the most recognized institutions in the block.

Are there government English medium schools in Paraspur?

Several primary schools under the Basic Shiksha Parishad in Paraspur have been designated as Model English Medium schools, in addition to private academies.

How can school administrations list or update their contact info?

School principals or administrators can contact the Paraspur.com editorial team via our Contact Page to submit official updates, admission schedules, or phone number corrections.
