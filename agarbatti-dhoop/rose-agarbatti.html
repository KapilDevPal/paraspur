Uses & Benefits of Rose Incense

Rose (Gulab) fragrance symbolizes divine love, devotion, and purity. It is the favored incense for worshipping Goddess Lakshmi, Goddess Durga, and Mother Mary. Lighting rose agarbatti in the evening creates a welcoming, festive atmosphere for guests and family.

  • Mood Elevation: Natural rose oil calms emotional stress and anxiety.
  • Ideal for Lakshmi Puja: Associated with prosperity and domestic harmony. See Lakshmi Puja Incense Guide.
