Difference Between Agarbatti and Dhoop

Understand the key differences between traditional agarbatti incense sticks and dhoop sticks/cones to choose the ideal incense for your home worship.

Side-by-Side Comparison Table

Feature Agarbatti (Incense Stick) Dhoop (Sticks / Cones)
Bamboo Core Contains a thin central bamboo stick 100% Bamboo-Free solid mass
Composition Wood powder & fragrance dipped Concentrated herbal paste & resins (Guggul/Loban)
Smoke Density Mild to medium gentle smoke Dense, fragrant smoke for large spaces
Burn Duration 40 to 50 minutes 60 to 75 minutes
