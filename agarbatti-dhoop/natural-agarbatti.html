100% Natural & Charcoal-Free Agarbatti

Breathe pure air during daily worship. Handcrafted from recycled temple flowers, wood powders, and pure essential oils—free from synthetic chemicals and toxic charcoal.

Why Choose Charcoal-Free Natural Incense?

Conventional cheap incense sticks rely on black charcoal powder and synthetic fragrance dip, which emit heavy black soot and chemical compounds when burned. Natural agarbatti uses flower powder (Phool), sawdust, and natural Jigatta gum for clean combustion.

🌱 Non-Irritating Smoke: Safe for elderly family members, children, and pets.
🌺 Flower Recycling: Made by upcycling holy temple flowers from rivers and mandirs.
