Famous Temples in Paraspur & Gonda

परसपुर और गोंडा जिले के प्रमुख, ऐतिहासिक एवं सुप्रसिद्ध धार्मिक स्थलों की विस्तृत जानकारी, असली तस्वीरें और गूगल मैप लोकेशन।

Real Photo • Chhapaiya
Chhapaiya, Gonda

Shree Swaminarayan Temple

Birthplace of Bhagwan Swaminarayan (1781 AD). A majestic, world-renowned temple complex featuring stunning marble architecture, holy lake (Narayan Sarovar), and spiritual heritage center.

View on Google Maps
Real Photo • 51 Shaktipeeth
Tulsipur, Devipatan Division

Maa Pateshwari Devi Patan

One of the holy 51 Shaktipeeths of Goddess Sati. Thousands of pilgrims visit during Navratri. Associated with Guru Gorakhnath and Karna of Mahabharata era.

View on Google Maps
Real Photo • Largest Shivling
Khargupur, Gonda

Prithvi Nath Mahadev

Houses the tallest Asian Shivling according to local tradition, established by Pandava prince Bhima during Mahabharata exile. Huge Shivratri festival destination.

View on Google Maps
Real Photo • Sukarkhet Sangam
Rajapur (Paska), Paraspur

Goswami Tulsi Das Temple

Located near Sukarkhet Paska Sangam (confluence of Sarayu & Ghaghra rivers), marking the birthplace of saint Goswami Tulsidas who composed Ramcharitmanas.

Raj Mandir Paraspur

Centrally located in Paraspur main market, serving as a key devotional hub and community gathering spot for festivals.

Shankar Mandir (Riyasat)
