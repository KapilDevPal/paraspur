Significance of Shri Durga Chalisa

Shri Durga Chalisa (श्री दुर्गा चालीसा) is a sacred 40-verse prayer dedicated to Maa Durga, the supreme personification of Shakti (Divine Power). Reciting Durga Chalisa during Navratri, Tuesdays, and Fridays grants inner courage, protects the household from distress, and brings prosperity and spiritual harmony.

Veneration of Navadurga (The Nine Manifestations)

1. Shailaputri
2. Brahmacharini
3. Chandraghanta
4. Kushmanda
5. Skandamata
6. Katyayani
7. Kalaratri
8. Mahagauri
9. Siddhidatri

Step-by-Step Durga Puja & Recitation Vidhi

1

Altar Setup & Kalash Sthapana

Clean the puja area. Spread a red cloth on a raised wooden platform. Place the idol or picture of Goddess Durga. Optionally set up Kalash with water, mango leaves, and coconut.

2

Offering Shodashopachara Items

Offer Kumkum (vermilion), Akshata (unbroken rice), red Hibiscus flowers, incense (Dhoop), and light a cow ghee lamp.

3

Chanting Durga Chalisa

Recite the 40 Chaupais of Shri Durga Chalisa with clear concentration and devotion. Offering Kheer, Halwa, or fruits as Bhog.

4

Performing Aarti

Conclude with Jai Ambe Gauri Aarti using camphor (Kapur) or a five-wick diya. Distribute Prasad to family and visitors.

Shri Durga Chalisa (Full Text & Meaning)

Opening Prayer (प्रारंभिक प्रार्थना)

नमो नमो दुर्गे सुख करनी। नमो नमो अंबा दुःख हरनी॥
निरंकार है ज्योति तुम्हारी। तिहूँ लोक फैली उजियारी॥

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Durga, who bestows supreme bliss. Salutations to Mother Amba, who dispels all sorrow. Your divine light is formless and eternal, illuminating all three worlds.

Verses 1 – 5

शशि ललाट मुख महाविशाला। नेत्र लाल भृकुटि विकराला॥१॥
रूप मातु को अधिक सुहावै। दरश करत जन अति सुख पावै॥२॥
तुम संसार शक्ति लय कीना। पालन हेतु अन्न धन दीना॥३॥
अन्नपूर्णा हुई जग पाला। तुम ही आदि सुंदरी बाला॥४॥

Meaning: The moon adorns your forehead, your countenance is radiant, and your three eyes shine with divine intensity. Your divine form brings immense happiness to devotees. You embody the energy of creation, sustenance, and dissolution. As Annapurna, you nourish all living beings.

Verses 6 – 10 (Slaying of Demons)

प्रलयकाल सब नाशनहारी। तुम ही शिव सुंदर पटरागी॥६॥
शिव जोगिनि भाषा सब गावैं। ब्रह्मा विष्णु तुम्हें नित ध्यावैं॥७॥
रूप सरस्वती को तुम धारा। दे बुद्धि विद्या बुद्धि सुधारा॥८॥
धर्यो रूप नरसिंह को अंबा। प्रगट भई फाड़कर खंबा॥९॥

Meaning: You are the destroyer of negative forces at cosmic dissolution, beloved consort of Lord Shiva. Brahma and Vishnu constantly meditate upon your power. You assume the form of Saraswati to bestow knowledge and wisdom, and manifest as Narasimha's Shakti to protect Prahlada.

Verses 35 – 40 (Protective Benediction)

शरण पड़े की लाज बचाओ। अरि दल दल बल तुरंत नशाओ॥३५॥
रिद्धि-सिद्धि देवौ धन धामा। जो ध्यावै जग में सुख पावै॥३६॥
दुर्गा चालीसा जो कोई गावै। सब सुख भोग परम पद पावै॥३७॥
देवीदास शरण निज जानी। करहु कृपा जगदंब भवानी॥३८॥

Meaning: O Mother! Protect those who seek refuge in you and destroy all harmful obstacles. Bestow spiritual wealth, prosperity, and peace. Whoever sings this Durga Chalisa enjoys earthly bliss and attains the highest spiritual goal.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Which days are most auspicious for Durga Chalisa recitation?

While daily recitation is highly beneficial, Fridays, Tuesdays, and all days during Chaitra & Sharad Navratri hold special significance.

Q2. What flowers are offered during Durga Puja?

Red Hibiscus (Gudhal), Red Rose, and Lotus are favored offerings for Maa Durga.
