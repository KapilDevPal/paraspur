Frequently Asked Questions on Mandi Bhav (अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल)

What is the Modal Price (मॉडल भाव क्या होता है)?

The Modal Price (मॉडल भाव) is the most common price at which the largest volume of a particular agricultural commodity is traded in a mandi on a given day. It is more representative than a simple average.

Which Mandis in Gonda district are covered by this API (गोंडा जिले की कौन-सी मंडियां शामिल हैं)?

Official data covers major Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs) in Gonda district including Colonelganj APMC (कर्नलगंज मंडी), Nawabganj APMC (नवाबगंज मंडी), and Gonda APMC (गोंडा मंडी), alongside surrounding regional markets in Bahraich, Basti, and Lucknow.

How frequently are Mandi prices updated (भाव कितनी बार अपडेट होते हैं)?

Mandi prices are updated daily by APMC market secretaries directly to the Ministry of Agriculture portal and pulled live via Paraspur.com's integration with data.gov.in.
