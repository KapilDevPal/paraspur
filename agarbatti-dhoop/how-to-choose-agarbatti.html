Incense Buyer's Guide

How to Choose the Best Agarbatti for Home & Puja

Key factors to consider when buying incense sticks: burn time, smoke density, charcoal vs herbal bases, and deity-specific fragrance notes.

4 Key Checklist Items for Buying Incense

1. Check for Charcoal-Free Base Opt for brown/beige herbal or flower-based sticks. Avoid pitch-black charcoal sticks that produce heavy black smoke. See our Natural Agarbatti Guide.
2. Match Aroma to Purpose Use Sandalwood for meditation, Mogra/Jasmine for Hanuman worship, and Rose for Lakshmi Puja.
3. Evaluate Burn Duration Standard 8-9 inch sticks should burn steadily for 45 to 60 minutes.
4. Bamboo vs Bamboo-Free (Dhoop) If avoiding bamboo during worship, choose solid Dhoop Sticks or Dhoop Cones.
