Significance of Hanuman Jayanti

Hanuman Jayanti marks the birth divine anniversary of Lord Hanuman, the eleventh Rudra avatar of Lord Shiva, born to Mata Anjani and Kesari by the grace of the Wind God (Vayu Deva). Celebrated on the full moon day (Purnima) of Chaitra month, this auspicious day is dedicated to selfless service, unwavering loyalty to Lord Rama, and spiritual strength.

In Northern India and regions around Gonda and Paraspur, Hanuman Jayanti is celebrated with grand temple processions, continuous recitation of Akhand Ramayana, Sundarkand, and 108 recitations of Shri Hanuman Chalisa.

Step-by-Step Instructions for Celebrating Hanuman Jayanti at Home

1

Morning Purification & Sankalpa

Rise before sunrise during Brahma Muhurta. Clean the puja room and wear red or orange traditional clothes. Take a pledge (Sankalpa) for peace and spiritual devotion.

2

Sindoor & Chameli Oil Offering (Chola Arpan)

Apply orange Sindoor mixed with Chameli (jasmine) oil to Lord Hanuman’s idol. This ritual (Chola) symbolizes complete surrender and grants protection.

3

Chanting Hanuman Chalisa & Sundarkand

Perform your complete Hanuman Puja Vidhi, then chant the Hanuman Chalisa 11 or 100 times alongside family members.

4

Offering Special Bhog & Distribution

Offer fresh Boondi, Besan Laddoos, Tulsi leaves, and Paan (betel leaf). Conclude with Hanuman Ji Ki Aarti and share Prasad with neighbors and devotees.
