About Paraspur.com

Paraspur.com is a dedicated digital information portal and comprehensive regional directory for Paraspur block and Gonda district, Uttar Pradesh.

Our Mission

Our mission is to organize and digitize essential local information for the residents, students, farmers, and business owners of Paraspur block and neighboring regions in Gonda district. By bridging the digital divide, we make critical details regarding educational institutions, healthcare centers, agricultural markets, public services, and historical heritage easily accessible to everyone.

01

Verified Directories

Accurate records of local schools, colleges, hospitals, clinics, and banks verified by local research.

02

Local Heritage

Documenting the rich cultural and spiritual legacy of Sukarkhet (Paska), Tulsidas birthplace, and Avadh history.

03

Community Growth

Promoting local startups, women entrepreneurs, markets, and daily Mandi Bhav for local farmers.

Editorial Guidelines & Quality Commitment

We adhere to strict content guidelines to ensure that information published on Paraspur.com is accurate, trustworthy, and helpful:

  • Data Verification: Directory listings and institutional details are cross-referenced with public administrative data and ground feedback.
  • Independence & Objectivity: Directory rankings and listings are published objectively without pay-for-rank manipulation.
  • User Feedback: We actively maintain a user submission channel to update outdated phone numbers, operating hours, or address changes promptly.
