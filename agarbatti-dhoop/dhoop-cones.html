Fast Fragrance Diffusion

Dhoop Cones (धूप कोन)

Compact, self-standing incense cones. Experience rapid fragrance release for morning rituals, meditation, and decorative backflow incense burners.

How to Use Dhoop Cones & Burn Duration

Dhoop cones feature a wide base tapering to a pointed tip. Because of their conical shape, the burning surface area increases as it burns downward, providing a richer fragrance release over 30 to 45 minutes.

🔸 Standard Dhoop Cones: Upward rising smoke, perfect for altar pedestals & home mandirs.
🌊 Backflow Waterfall Cones: Hollow center creates mesmerizing downward cascading smoke.
