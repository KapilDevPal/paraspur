Banking & Credit Services for Paraspur

Frequently Asked Questions: Banking in Paraspur

What is the IFSC code for State Bank of India (SBI) Paraspur branch?

The verified IFSC code for SBI Paraspur is SBIN0012497. This is required for RTGS, NEFT, and IMPS money transfers.

Are there 24-hour working ATMs available in Paraspur town?

Yes, the SBI ATM near the Block Office and Indian Bank ATM at Main Market Colonelganj Road operate round-the-clock for cash withdrawals.

How can farmers apply for Kisan Credit Card (KCC) in Paraspur?

Farmers can visit the agricultural desk at Indian Bank or SBI Paraspur with land revenue records (Khatauni), Aadhaar card, and passport-size photographs to initiate KCC applications.
