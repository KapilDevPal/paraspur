Fostering Rural Innovation in Awadh

Rural entrepreneurship in Paraspur Block is experiencing rapid evolution. Leveraging digital connectivity, local youth and women artisans are establishing micro-enterprises ranging from organic agricultural processing and soil health consulting to digital services, traditional handicraft exports, and hyper-local supply logistics.

🌾
Agri-Tech Initiative

Saryu Soil & Crop Care

A local agricultural consultation startup assisting sugarcane and wheat farmers across Paraspur block with soil pH testing, organic bio-fertilizer recommendations, and micro-irrigation setups.

Sector: Agriculture & Soil Health
🧵
Women Empowerment

Avadh Craft Collective

A self-help collective empowering women in local Paraspur villages to craft hand-embroidered chikankari fabrics, eco-friendly jute bags, and traditional festive decorations for state exhibitions.

Sector: Handicrafts & Textiles
🚚
Digital Supply Chain

Paraspur Direct Express

A tech-enabled logistics connection linking local Mandi produce and retail shops in Paraspur with regional buyers in Colonelganj and Gonda headquarters.

Sector: Logistics & Rural Transport

Government Schemes for Local Entrepreneurs

Budding entrepreneurs in Paraspur and Gonda district can access institutional funding and mentorship through government programs:

💼 PM Mudra Yojana (Shishu, Kishore, Tarun) Collateral-free loans up to ₹10 Lakh for micro-enterprises, retail shops, small manufacturing units, and service businesses.
🏛️ UP Startup Policy 2026 Seed capital grants, incubation support, and monthly sustenance allowances for registered innovative technology startups in Uttar Pradesh.
