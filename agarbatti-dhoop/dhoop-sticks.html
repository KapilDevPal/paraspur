Bamboo-Free Pure Resins

Natural Dhoop Sticks (धूप बत्ती)

Solid herbal incense sticks without bamboo cores. Concentrated with ayurvedic herbs, pure Guggul, Frankincense, and Cow Ghee for deep air purification.

Why Choose Bamboo-Free Dhoop Sticks?

Traditional scriptures caution against burning bamboo during religious ceremonies. Dhoop sticks are crafted purely from aromatic wood powders, herbs, and tree resins (Guggul & Loban) without any bamboo core stick.

🔥 Dense & Slow Burning: Burn duration of 60 to 75 minutes per stick.
🍃 100% Herbal & Resin Rich: Emits rich protective smoke ideal for large living rooms & temples.

How to Use Dhoop Sticks

  1. Place the dhoop stick vertically into the ceramic or brass holder provided.
  2. Light the top tip until it catches a flame, then gently blow out the flame leaving a glowing red ember.
  3. Allow the rich fragrant smoke to spread across your mandir and home premises.
